# HEY IT'S ME — Photobooth

> A fun photobooth web app by **Deep & Wide · One Love**

## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Auto-capture** — select a timer (3, 5, or 10 sec) and it shoots all 4 photos automatically
- 🎨 **2 Photostrip Templates** — Version 1 (branding at bottom) or Version 2 (branding in middle)
- 💾 **Save your strip** — download your completed photostrip as a JPG
- 🌑 **Minimalist dark aesthetic** — warm gold & cream palette

## 📖 How to Use

1. Click **START**
2. Pick your **photostrip design** (Version 1 or 2)
3. Choose your **countdown timer** (3 / 5 / 10 seconds) — this starts immediately!
4. Strike 4 poses — photos are taken automatically
5. **Save** your photostrip or **Retake**
